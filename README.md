# Attendance Tracker

A modern, lightweight web application to track and monitor your college attendance across multiple subjects.

## Features

✨ **Track Multiple Subjects** - Easily manage attendance for all your courses  
📊 **Live Statistics** - View overall attendance percentage at a glance  
💾 **Auto-Save** - Data persists in your browser using localStorage  
🎨 **Beautiful UI** - Modern glassmorphism design with smooth animations  
📱 **Responsive Design** - Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices  

## Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Click "Add Subject" to create a new subject
3. Enter subject details (name, code, present & absent classes)
4. Track your attendance as you attend classes
5. Your data is automatically saved locally

## How to Use

- **Add Subject**: Click the "+ Add Subject" button in the top-right
- **Edit Attendance**: Click any subject card to update your attendance numbers
- **Delete Subject**: Swipe or click the delete button on a subject card
- **View Stats**: Check the overview card for your semester attendance summary

## Data

All your data is stored locally in your browser. No internet connection required after the first load. Data persists across browser sessions.

## Technologies

- **HTML5** - Semantic markup
- **CSS3** - Modern styling with animations
- **Vanilla JavaScript** - No dependencies required
